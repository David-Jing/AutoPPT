### Summary

The contract meets the stage's structure and honours the owner's answers, and the wording choice autoppt=slides is acceptable. It is NOT-READY because eight gaps would make a developer guess or fail the requirements: the setup-file checks cannot test unsaved keys, the slide-writing retry is not safe, the failure table contradicts FR12.3 and C11, half-built files and hymn backgrounds cannot be identified, several port types are undefined, and time limits are missing. Fix R-01 to R-08 before the SlidePlan shape is frozen.
