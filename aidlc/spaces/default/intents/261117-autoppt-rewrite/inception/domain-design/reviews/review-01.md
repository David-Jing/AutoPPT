### Summary

The component catalogue is well-formed (unique names, symmetric edges, single owners, no component cycle), the ADRs are complete, and the Q1 to Q8 answers and naming rules are honoured. It is NOT-READY because of three Major gaps: the core-versus-other-module dependency direction is unstated and contradicted by ConnectionMonitor's HTTPS call, the FR14.5 all-or-nothing reload across LibrarySync and ServiceBuilder has no owner, and four template or configuration data needs have no edge. All three are small edits to components.md and decisions.md.
