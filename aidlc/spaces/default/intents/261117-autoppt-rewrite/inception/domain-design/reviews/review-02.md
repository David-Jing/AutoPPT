> R-01 Reviewer note: Ports table lists ten core-owned ports. ADR-001 and the intro state the one-way direction. I derived the module graph from the Module column and found no google, esv or library edge to another of those three, and no core component depending on a non-core component except through a named port (list under Validation). The HTTPS probe sits in google and ConnectionMonitor keeps only timing and events. Remaining Minor issues are in New findings.

> R-02 Reviewer note: ReloadAllHymns (core) is declared with edges to LibrarySync (port) and ServiceBuilder. Its text says swap and clear only on full success, nothing changes on failure. ServiceBuilder and LibrarySync describe the same split, and ADR-003 matches. The story map for US14.3 was not updated; see the new Minor finding.

> R-03 Reviewer note: Configuration supplies the template id (MakeSlidesRun to Configuration edge). MakeSlidesRun calls TemplateReader for the FR12.6 check. TemplateReader to SlideEditors (schedule roles) and to HymnLayout (background pool) edges exist in the yaml, the diagram and the summary table, and are symmetric.

> R-04 Reviewer note: MakeSlidesRun behaviour and ADR-005 treat a connection error from SlideWriter or SlidesCleaner as the lost-connection stop with remaining rows Skipped.

> R-05 Reviewer note: GoogleAuth owns sign out and depends on SecretStore. ScreenViews depends on GoogleAuth for sign out. SecretStore still lists "Sign out of the Google token" as a responsibility; this is consistent because it is the removal it performs when asked.

> R-06 Reviewer note: Entity is SettingsFile in the yaml and the Entity Ownership table. UserSettings now names only the component.

> R-07 Reviewer note: traceability.json maps US3.5 to "SingleInstanceGuard, MakeSlidesRun" and the note says so.

### Summary

The seven fixes are in place and the graph holds: acyclic, symmetric, no sibling-module edges, and core reaches other modules only through declared ports. The one Major finding is a missing sentence about where the types that cross ports are defined, which a developer needs to keep core free of other modules but which does not change the component structure. With one Major and no Critical findings, the verdict is READY.
